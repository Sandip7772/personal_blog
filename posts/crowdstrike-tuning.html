Before you add an exclusion

An exclusion hides a behaviour, not just a false alarm. I always confirm what the process does and who owns it first.

  1. Confirm the detection is benign with the system owner.
  2. Scope the exclusion as narrowly as possible.
  3. Record why it was added and review it every quarter.
A tuned alert you understand is worth more than a silent one you don't.
