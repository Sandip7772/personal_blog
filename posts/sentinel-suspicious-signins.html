Hunting suspicious sign-ins in Microsoft Sentinel

This is a dummy post used to preview how a real article will look. Replace this text with your own writing.

Why start with sign-ins

Most incidents I see begin with an identity. A quick morning review of sign-in activity catches problems before they spread.

Start with the questions you ask every day, then turn them into queries.

The query

SigninLogs
| where TimeGenerated > ago(24h)
| where ResultType != 0
| summarize Failures = count() by UserPrincipalName, IPAddress
| where Failures > 10
| order by Failures desc

What I check next

  • Is the IP address new for this user?
  • Did a successful sign-in follow the failures?
  • Is MFA enforced for the account?
